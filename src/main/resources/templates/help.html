Quick Start Guide
Making a Reservation
  1. Go to Reservations → New Booking
  2. Enter guest details and dates
  3. Select available room
  4. Review and confirm booking
Check-in Process
  1. Find reservation in list
  2. Click Check In button
  3. Verify guest identity
  4. Room key is issued
Generating Bills
  1. Go to reservation details
  2. Click Generate Bill
  3. Review charges
  4. Record payment
Check-out Process
  1. Ensure bill is settled
  2. Click Check Out
  3. Print final invoice
  4. Room becomes available
Frequently Asked Questions
Navigate to Reservations → New Booking from the sidebar. Fill in the guest's details, select the check-in and check-out dates, choose an available room, and click Confirm Booking. You'll receive a reservation number upon successful creation.
Go to Reservations, find the reservation, and click the Check In button. The reservation status will change to "CHECKED_IN" and the room will be marked as occupied.
Yes! You can modify reservations that are in "CONFIRMED" status. Go to the reservation detail page and click Edit. You can change dates, room assignment, and guest details. Note: already checked-in reservations require manager approval to modify.
Bills are generated automatically when a reservation is checked out, or you can create one manually from the reservation detail page. To print, go to Bills → View Bill → Print. The print-friendly version opens in a new window.
Managers can apply discounts from the bill detail page. Enter the discount percentage and reason in the Apply Discount section. The bill total will be automatically recalculated.
The system provides Monthly Reports (revenue, occupancy, booking trends), Weekly Reports (short-term performance), and a Reports Dashboard with KPIs, charts, and analytics. All reports can be printed.
  • Customer – Can view/manage their own reservations and bills
  • Staff – Can manage all reservations, check-in/out, and generate bills
  • Manager – Full access: room management, reports, discounts, user management
