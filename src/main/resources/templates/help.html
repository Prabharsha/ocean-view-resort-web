Quick Start Guide
Making a Reservation
  1. Go to Reservations → New Booking
  2. Enter guest details and dates
  3. Select an available room
  4. Review and confirm booking
Check-in Process
  1. Open the reservation
  2. Click Check In button
  3. Choose payment method (POS / Cash)
  4. Optionally redeem loyalty points
  5. Enter payment reference & confirm
Billing & Payment
  1. Bill is auto-generated on check-in
  2. Apply loyalty-points discount if eligible
  3. Record payment (Card / Cash)
  4. Print or email the receipt
Check-out Process
  1. Confirm bill is settled
  2. Click Check Out
  3. Print final invoice
  4. Room status returns to Available
Loyalty Program

Guests earn points on every stay. Points unlock tier benefits and can be redeemed for bill discounts.

Tier Points Required Benefits
🥉 Bronze 0 – 499 pts 5% room discount
🥈 Silver 500 – 1,999 pts 10% room discount + free breakfast
🥇 Gold 2,000 – 4,999 pts 15% discount + breakfast + late checkout
💎 Platinum 5,000+ pts 20% discount + all Gold benefits + room upgrade
Earning Points
  • Points are awarded automatically after each completed stay
  • Earning rate depends on room type and length of stay
  • Bonus points may be granted by managers for special occasions
Redeeming Points
  • 1 pt = LKR 0.10 off the bill
  • Redeem during the check-in payment step
  • Use Use Max Points to apply the maximum allowed
  • Remaining balance is charged via POS or Cash
Payment Processing

Payments are collected at check-in. The system supports two payment methods.

POS / Card
  • Select POS in the payment modal
  • Enter the POS terminal transaction reference (e.g. TXN-2026-00123)
  • Reference is stored against the bill for audit
Cash
  • Select Cash in the payment modal
  • Enter the front-desk cash receipt number (e.g. RCT-2026-00045)
  • Receipt number is recorded on the bill
The check-in flow automatically: (1) marks the reservation as Checked In, (2) generates the bill, (3) applies any loyalty-point discount, and (4) records the payment — all in a single step.

Frequently Asked Questions
Navigate to Reservations → New Booking from the sidebar. Fill in the guest's details, select the check-in and check-out dates, choose an available room, and click Confirm Booking. A unique reservation number is issued upon successful creation.
Open the reservation and click Check In. A payment modal will appear — select POS or Cash, enter a payment reference number, optionally redeem loyalty points, then click Confirm Check-In. The system will automatically mark the reservation as Checked In, generate the bill, apply any point discount, and record the payment.
Yes. Reservations in PENDING or CONFIRMED status can be edited. Go to the reservation detail page and click Edit. You can change dates, room assignment, and guest details. Reservations that are already checked-in require manager approval to modify.
Bills are generated automatically at check-in. You can also create one manually from the reservation detail page via Bill Summary → View Full Bill. To print, open the bill and click the Print button — a print-friendly view opens automatically.
Managers can apply a percentage discount from the bill detail page. Enter the discount percentage in the Apply Discount section and provide a reason. The bill total recalculates immediately. Loyalty-point redemptions during check-in are converted to a discount automatically.
Guests accumulate loyalty points with every completed stay. Points determine their tier (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum), each unlocking better discounts and perks. At check-in, staff can redeem points against the bill — 1 point = LKR 0.10 discount. Use the Use Max Points button to apply the highest allowable redemption. Tier and balance are always visible in the payment modal.
The system supports two payment methods at check-in:
  • POS (Card) – Enter the transaction reference from the POS terminal (e.g. TXN-2026-00123).
  • Cash – Enter the cash receipt number issued by the front-desk cashier (e.g. RCT-2026-00045).
Both reference numbers are saved against the bill for audit purposes.
Three report types are available under Reports in the sidebar:
  • Monthly Reports – Revenue, occupancy, and booking trends for any month. Supports PDF download.
  • Weekly Reports – Short-term performance by ISO week number.
  • Reports Dashboard – Live KPIs, interactive charts, and a full analytics overview.
  • Customer – Can view and manage their own reservations, bills, and loyalty balance.
  • Staff – Can manage all reservations, perform check-in/check-out, generate bills, and process payments.
  • Manager – Full access: room management, discount application, reports, user management, and all Staff capabilities.
Open the reservation and click the Cancel button (visible for PENDING and CONFIRMED reservations). A confirmation prompt will appear. Once cancelled, the room becomes available again and the reservation status is updated to CANCELLED. Checked-in reservations cannot be cancelled directly — contact a manager.
Room status is updated automatically: a room becomes Occupied when a guest checks in, and Available again after check-out or cancellation. Staff can also change room status manually from Rooms → Room Detail (e.g. to mark it as Under Maintenance).
